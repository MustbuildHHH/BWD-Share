Closet wall

LineSizePrice
Bedroom built-ins15'-0"$26,000

As drawn: 171½" wide, floor to ceiling, 6 glass doors with LED, hanging space and 6 drawers.

Octolux 18 matt black frame, Optiwhite glassMTL 1 + GLS 1 · glass doors

Our recommendations

  • More drawers along the bottom, either concealed behind the main doors or showing as a drawer band

  • Closet, library, desk, display or TV wall?
  • Height and depth; drawers full span or one per door
