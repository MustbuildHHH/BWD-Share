Bed wall

  • Two-layer leather headboard. The back layer is 48″ high with an oak cap, stands 1″ off the wall for an LED glow, and curves around the outside end of each nightstand.
  • The second layer is a 1″ leather layer, 44″ high, running the full length and wrapping the ends of the nightstands.
  • Both layers hang on a French cleat.
  • Painted panel trim above. A Kuzco Huron pendant in brushed gold hangs from the ceiling over each nightstand.
  • Pendant switch and an outlet with USB-C built into the headboard above each nightstand.
  • Upholstered bed floating on a recessed 1 ½″ base, upholstered footboard.

Ceiling

  • Perimeter band of ¾″ plywood strapping and ½″ gypsum, dropped 1 3⁄16″, with a NISA-KRA trimless LED in its inner edge behind a ⅛″ reveal.
  • Fabi custom cove directly under the band, 3 1⁄16″ down from the ceiling overall. Stops square at the built-ins.
  • Pendant canopies mount to the band, 9″ off the bed wall.

Built-ins

  • Lacquered cream (graphite as an option) with brushed brass pulls and slim shaker fronts.
  • Hanging cabinets left and right with double doors. Closet lights switch on when the doors open, by pressure switch.
  • Open shelves in the centre with a hewn wood tongue and groove back and LED lights in the underside of each shelf. Shelves in graphite or oak.
  • Oak counter at 35″. Three rows of drawers below on a 4″ kick.
  • ½″ shadow reveal at the ceiling.

To confirm

  • Window height assumed at 42″ to 90″ off the floor. Wood-lined jamb with a shadow gap, no casing.
  • Pendants hang 9″ off the bed wall, loop at 61 ½″ to 85 ½″ off the floor as drawn.
  • King mattress assumed, 76″ × 80″.
  • Lit closet rod in the hanging cabinets, to ask Krys.
  • Window jamb finish, leather tone or black.
  • Finished ceiling height to be site measured after flooring.
  • Panel trim frames lowered to top out at 103″ so they clear the cove, which meets the wall at about 105″. Confirm the new frame height.
  • Band width taken as 14 ¾″ off the wall from the section. Band and cove assumed to run the full perimeter except the built-in wall.
