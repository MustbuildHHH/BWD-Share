Material and finish

White oak, light Brushed brass Leather, top and headboard
  • Oak to match the closet insert, with a fine vertical reed that follows the curve.
  • The top has a recessed leather insert inside a 1″ oak border, to match the headboard.
  • The curved body is built from narrow solid staves, or a bending-ply core under oak veneer.
  • The base is set back and finished dark, so the table appears to float like the bed.

Drawers

  • Two drawers on soft-close Blum slides. Boxes are about 8″ deep to clear the curved back.
  • Slab fronts that follow the curve, reeded to match the body or smooth. Both are shown above.
  • Outlets and charging are built into the headboard, not the table.
  • The table fixes to the headboard so it cannot tip when a drawer is open.
